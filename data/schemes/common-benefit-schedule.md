# Common Benefit Schedule

These amounts are the **official, unified schedule that applies to registered workers across
all 17 Unorganised Workers Welfare Boards** (Construction, Manual Workers, Drivers, Washermen,
Domestic Workers, etc.) — not just one specific board — except where a benefit is explicitly
marked as board-specific below.

## Accident relief

*விபத்து மரணம் (accidental death), விபத்து இழப்பீடு (accident compensation), கணவர்/மனைவி விபத்தில்
இறந்தால் (if spouse dies in an accident), ஊனம் (disability) — this section covers accident
death, disability, and compensation amounts.*

| Benefit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Accidental death, anywhere | ₹1,00,000 |
| Accidental death during course of employment at a work site — **Construction Workers board only** | ₹5,00,000 |
| Accidental disability (based on extent of disability) | up to ₹1,00,000 |

## Death and funeral

*இயற்கை மரணம் (natural death), இறுதிச் சடங்கு உதவி (funeral assistance) — natural (non-accident)
death and funeral expense amounts.*

| Benefit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Natural death assistance | ₹20,000 |
| Funeral expenses assistance | ₹5,000 |

## Marriage assistance

*திருமண உதவி (marriage assistance) — amount for male and female workers.*

| For | Amount |
|---|---|
| Male worker | ₹3,000 |
| Female worker | ₹5,000 |

## Maternity assistance (miscarriage / medical termination of pregnancy)

*மகப்பேறு உதவி, கருச்சிதைவு உதவி (maternity / miscarriage assistance).*

| Case | Amount |
|---|---|
| Miscarriage | ₹6,000 |
| Medical termination of pregnancy | ₹3,000 |

## Pension

*ஓய்வூதியம் (monthly pension), குடும்ப ஓய்வூதியம் (family pension).*

| Benefit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Monthly pension (registered worker) | ₹1,000/month |
| Family pension — **Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board only**, paid to family if the registered worker passes away | ₹500/month |

Note: family pension is **not** a general benefit across all 17 boards — it is specific to the
Construction Workers board. A driver, domestic worker, or other non-construction unorganised
worker's registered board does not currently offer this as of the last verified date.

## Other assistance

| Benefit | Amount |
|---|---|
| Reimbursement of spectacles | up to ₹500 |

## Educational assistance

| For | Amount |
|---|---|
| Girl child studying 10th | ₹1,000 |
| Passed 10th | ₹1,000 |
| Girl child studying 11th | ₹1,000 |
| Girl child studying 12th | ₹1,500 |
| Passed 12th | ₹1,500 |
| Regular degree course (with hostel facility) | ₹1,500 (₹1,750 with hostel) |
| Regular postgraduate course (with hostel facility) | ₹4,000 (₹5,000 with hostel) |
| Professional degree course (with hostel facility) | ₹4,000 (₹6,000 with hostel) |
| Professional postgraduate course (with hostel facility) | ₹6,000 (₹8,000 with hostel) |
| ITI or Polytechnic (with hostel facility) | ₹1,000 (₹1,200 with hostel) |

## How benefits are paid

All welfare scheme assistance is transferred **directly to the beneficiary's bank account**
through RBI's Electronic Clearance System (RECS) — there is no cash payout at the Labour Office.

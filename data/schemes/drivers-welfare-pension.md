# Pension & Insurance for Auto/Taxi Drivers in Tamil Nadu

Auto, taxi, and lorry drivers register under the **Manual Workers Welfare Board** (one of the 18
TNUWWB boards) as unorganised-sector workers. A separate **Tamil Nadu Auto Rickshaw and Taxi
Drivers Welfare Board** also exists and the state government has allocated dedicated funds
(reported around ₹50 crore in a recent budget) specifically for auto/taxi driver welfare —
drivers should check with their district Labour Office which board their registration falls
under, since benefit tables can differ slightly.

## Current confirmed benefits (Manual Workers board, applies to registered drivers)

- **Monthly pension at age 60+:** ₹1,000/month
- **Family pension** (if the registered driver passes away): ₹500/month to surviving family
- **Accident insurance:** ₹2,00,000 for accidental death, ₹1,00,000 for permanent disability
  (same as general Manual Workers board benefits — see `manual-workers-board-benefits.md`)

## Known advocacy / pending changes (not yet enacted — context only)

- Driver unions have publicly requested the pension be raised from ₹1,000/month to ₹3,000/month.
  As of the last verified date, **₹1,000/month remains the official confirmed amount** — do not
  tell a user the higher figure is in effect.
  requested a festival (Deepavali) bonus of ₹5,000/driver through the welfare board; this is a
  request, not a confirmed disbursed benefit.

## Why this matters for eligibility answers

If a user identifies as an auto/taxi/lorry driver, they should be pointed to:
1. Register (or confirm registration) with the **Manual Workers Welfare Board** via
   `www.tnuwwb.tn.gov.in` or their district e-Sevai centre.
2. The general Manual Workers benefit table in `manual-workers-board-benefits.md`.
3. A note that a dedicated Auto Rickshaw and Taxi Drivers Welfare Board also exists with
   state-allocated funds, and they should ask their district Labour Office which board applies to
   them, since this area has had recent policy changes.

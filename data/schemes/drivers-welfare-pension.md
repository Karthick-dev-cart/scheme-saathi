# Tamil Nadu Unorganised Drivers Welfare Board

**Auto, taxi, and lorry drivers register under their own dedicated board — the Tamil Nadu
Unorganised Drivers Welfare Board — not under the Manual Workers board.** This is confirmed by
the official Labour Department citizen's charter, which lists it as board #3 of the 17
Unorganised Workers Welfare Boards.

## Benefits

No board-specific additions beyond the common schedule are documented for this board in the
official source. Registered drivers receive the full `common-benefit-schedule.md`: monthly
pension ₹1,000 at age 60+, accidental death ₹1,00,000 (the ₹5,00,000 work-site rate is
construction-only), natural death ₹20,000, funeral ₹5,000, marriage/maternity/education
assistance as listed there. **Family pension (₹500/month to surviving family) is a Construction
Workers board-only benefit and does not apply to the Drivers board**, based on the official
source — do not tell a driver they qualify for it.

## Eligibility

- Tamil Nadu resident, age 18–60
- Working as an auto, taxi, or lorry driver in the unorganised sector
- Not already covered under EPF or ESI

## Unconfirmed / pending items — do not state as current policy

These come from news reporting on state budget allocations and driver-union advocacy, **not**
from the official citizen's charter, and should be presented to users as unconfirmed:

- News reports describe a state budget allocation (reported around ₹50 crore) for auto/taxi
  driver welfare, and mention of a possibly separate "Auto Rickshaw and Taxi Drivers Welfare
  Board." It is unclear from available sources whether this is a distinct body from the
  Unorganised Drivers Welfare Board above, or the same board under a different informal name —
  **flag this ambiguity to the user rather than asserting either way**.
- Driver unions have publicly requested the pension be raised from ₹1,000/month to ₹3,000/month.
  **₹1,000/month remains the only confirmed amount.**
- Driver unions have also requested a festival (Deepavali) bonus of ₹5,000/driver; this is a
  request, not a confirmed disbursed benefit.

## What to tell a driver asking about eligibility

1. Register (or confirm registration) with the **Unorganised Drivers Welfare Board** at the
   district Assistant Commissioner of Labour (Social Security Scheme) office — not the Manual
   Workers board.
2. Expect the common benefit schedule; do not promise family pension.
3. If they ask about a pension increase or festival bonus they heard about, clarify it is a
   proposal/request, not yet confirmed policy, and suggest they confirm current status with
   their district Labour office since this area has seen recent advocacy activity.

# Tamil Nadu Unorganised Workers Welfare Boards

Under the **Tamil Nadu Manual Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Work) Act,
1982**, the Tamil Nadu government has constituted **17 separate Unorganised Workers Welfare
Boards**, each covering a distinct occupational group and each administering its own
registration, though most share a common benefit schedule (see
`common-benefit-schedule.md`).

## The 17 boards

1. Tamil Nadu Construction Workers Welfare Board
2. Tamil Nadu Manual Workers Welfare Board
3. Tamil Nadu Unorganised Drivers Welfare Board — **this is the board auto/taxi/lorry drivers
   register under, not the Manual Workers board**
4. Tamil Nadu Washermen Welfare Board
5. Tamil Nadu Hair Dressers Welfare Board
6. Tamil Nadu Tailoring Workers Welfare Board
7. Tamil Nadu Handicraft Workers Welfare Board
8. Tamil Nadu Palm Tree Workers Welfare Board
9. Tamil Nadu Handloom and Handloom Silk Weaving Workers Welfare Board
10. Tamil Nadu Footwear and Leather Goods Manufactory and Tannery Workers Welfare Board
11. Tamil Nadu Artists Welfare Board
12. Tamil Nadu Goldsmiths Welfare Board
13. Tamil Nadu Pottery Workers Welfare Board
14. Tamil Nadu Domestic Workers Welfare Board
15. Tamil Nadu Powerloom Weaving Workers Welfare Board
16. Tamil Nadu Street Vending and Shops and Establishments Workers Welfare Board
17. Tamil Nadu Cooking Food Workers Welfare Board

Construction Workers (board 1) and Manual Workers (board 2) are the two largest/oldest boards;
the other 15, including the Drivers board, were constituted afterward under separate welfare
schemes but administered through the same district machinery.

## Important: this is different from the Tamil Nadu Labour Welfare Board

The **Tamil Nadu Labour Welfare Board** (under the Tamil Nadu Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1972) is a
**separate body for organised-sector workers** — factories, plantations, motor transport
undertakings, shops, and catering establishments. Its benefit amounts (e.g. marriage assistance
₹10,000, scholarships ₹4,000–₹12,000) are **different from, and not applicable to**, the 17
Unorganised Workers Welfare Boards described here. If a user has a formal employer who deducts
PF/ESI, they likely fall under this organised-sector board instead — do not quote unorganised-board
figures to them.

## Common eligibility (all 17 unorganised-worker boards)

- Tamil Nadu resident, age **18–60**
- Working in the specific occupational category that board covers
- Generally **not already covered under EPF or ESI** (this is the core distinction from the
  organised-sector Labour Welfare Board)

## Administration

Each of the 32 districts has an **Assistant Commissioner of Labour (Social Security Scheme)**
office (35 offices total) that handles registration, renewal, and disbursement of welfare
assistance for all 17 boards. Registration uses a web-based application that captures a photo
via webcam and issues a system-generated photo ID card. All benefit payments are transferred
directly to the beneficiary's bank account via RBI's Electronic Clearance System (RECS) —
**there is no cash disbursement**.

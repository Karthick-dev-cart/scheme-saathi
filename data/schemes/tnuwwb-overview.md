# Tamil Nadu Unorganised Workers Welfare Board (TNUWWB)

TNUWWB is the umbrella body under the Tamil Nadu Department of Labour that administers social
security for workers in the unorganised/informal sector who are **not covered by EPF or ESI**.
It functions through **18 separate welfare boards**, broadly split into two groups:

- **Construction Workers Welfare Board** — covers 53–54 construction-related trades (masons,
  carpenters, electricians, welders, painters, plumbers, etc.)
- **Manual Workers Welfare Board & 16 others** — covers around 61–70 other informal occupations,
  including **drivers (auto, taxi, lorry)**, domestic workers, street vendors, barbers,
  washermen (dhobis), sanitation workers, handloom workers, cobblers, and small manufacturing
  trades.

## Who is eligible to register (common to all 18 boards)

- Permanent resident of Tamil Nadu
- Age between **18 and 60 years**
- Actively engaged in one of the listed occupational categories
- **Not already covered** under EPF (Employees' Provident Fund) or ESI (Employees' State
  Insurance)
- Monthly income generally below ₹15,000 (criterion used by the Manual Workers board; construction
  board eligibility is occupation-based rather than income-based)

## Required documents at enrolment

- Aadhaar card
- Proof of Tamil Nadu residence/address
- Self-declaration of occupation, OR employment verification from an employer, contractor,
  government body, registered trade union, or Village Administrative Officer (VAO)
- Identity proof (driving licence, voter ID, school certificate, or medical practitioner
  certificate, depending on trade)
- Aadhaar-linked bank passbook (first page)
- Family card / ration card

## Registration process

1. Visit the nearest **Labour Department office** or an **e-Sevai centre**, or register online at
   the relevant board portal using a valid mobile number.
2. Complete the unorganised worker registration form; verify via OTP for online applications.
3. Pay the **annual registration fee (commonly ₹30)**.
4. Submit supporting documents.
5. Receive a registration acknowledgment (SMS for online applications).
6. On approval, receive a **Permanent Registration Number / ID card**.
7. **Renewal is required every 5 years** until age 60 — apply for renewal **one month before
   expiry**. Only members with a current, unexpired registration can claim benefits.

There is **no fee** for registration, renewal, petitions, or benefit claim submissions beyond the
annual registration fee noted above.

## Important note on benefit amounts

Benefit amounts (marriage assistance, maternity assistance, accident/death compensation, pension,
education assistance) **differ between the specific welfare board a worker is registered under**
(e.g. Construction Workers board vs. Manual Workers board). Always check the board-specific
document for exact figures — see `manual-workers-board-benefits.md` and
`construction-workers-board-benefits.md`.
